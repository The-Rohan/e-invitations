Questions

Answered before you ask.

How do digital wedding invitations work?+

You customise a design from your own panel, then share a link. Each guest can be given a private link showing their name, the functions they are invited to, and how many seats are held for them.

Can I send different guests different functions?+

Yes. Tick which functions each guest is invited to and they see only those. Somebody invited to the Walima alone never sees the Baraat details.

Can I change details after sending the invitation?+

Yes, at any time during your access period. Every link you have already shared updates instantly, so nobody is left with an old venue.

Do my guests need an app or an account?+

No. They tap the link and it opens in their browser. That is the whole experience.
