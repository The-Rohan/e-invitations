Privacy notice

What we collect, why we collect it, and how long we keep it. Written plainly, because you are trusting us with your family's details.

1. What we collect

  • Your account: name, email address, password (stored hashed, never readable by us) and a phone number if you give one.
  • Your card: everything you type or upload into it, including names, dates, venues, photographs and music.
  • Your guests: the names, headcounts and, if you add them, phone numbers you enter into your guest list.
  • Replies: when a guest answers on a card, their name, whether they are coming, how many, and any message they write. Only the card's owner sees them.
  • Orders: what you bought, the amount, and the transaction reference you give us for a bank transfer. We never see your bank login or card details.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form, WhatsApp or email.

2. What we use it for

We do not sell data, we do not run advertising on invitations, and we do not contact your guests for our own purposes.

  • To host and show your invitation to the people you send it to.
  • To activate your purchase and answer your questions.
  • To tell you about your own order or card, such as when access is about to end.
  • To keep the service working and to spot abuse.

3. Who can see an invitation

A general link can be opened by anybody who has it. A private guest link is unguessable but not secret: anyone it is forwarded to can open it. Please keep off a card anything you would not want forwarded. Cards are marked so search engines do not index them.

4. Cookies and analytics

One cookie keeps you signed in to your panel. It is essential and is set only when you log in.

Our marketing pages use Google Analytics to count visits and Meta Pixel to measure advertising performance. These services receive page visits and browser/device information and may set cookies, but load only after you accept them in the cookie banner. You can say no, and your choice is remembered on your device. To change your choice, clear this site’s browser storage and choose again. Invitation pages, previews and your panel do not load these trackers.

5. How long we keep it

Your card and guest list are kept for as long as your account exists, including after your access period ends, so you can extend without starting over. If you delete a card, its guest list, replies and uploads are deleted with it. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete everything within 30 days, except records of payment we are required to keep.

6. Where it is stored

On servers we control. Uploads are stored privately and served only through your card's links.

7. Your rights

You can see and change everything in your panel. To export or delete your data, or to ask anything about this notice, write to support@dawatnama.store.

This text is written to match how the product actually works. It is not legal advice; have it reviewed before relying on it commercially.
