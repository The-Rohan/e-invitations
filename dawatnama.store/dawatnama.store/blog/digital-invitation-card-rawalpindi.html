Digital invitation card in Rawalpindi: online shadi card ki guide (راولپنڈی)

Pindi ke Saddar, Satellite Town aur GT Road ke marquees ke liye online shadi card: sahi address, waqt, aur bari biradri tak ek link.

Rawalpindi ki shadi mein biradri bari hoti hai aur rasmein poori: Dholki, Mehndi, Baraat, Walima. 'Online shadi card Rawalpindi' dhoondne wale aksar woh khandan hain jinke mehman Pindi, Islamabad, Chakwal aur Jhelum mein phaile hain aur jinhein har ghar card pohanchana mushkil lagta hai.

Rawalpindi mein shadi ka season aur venues

Pindi ke marquees aur shadi halls Saddar, Satellite Town, Chaklala, Peshawar Road aur GT Road par hain, aur purane shehar mein ghar ki galiyon mein Mehndi aaj bhi hoti hai. Murree Road ka traffic shaam ko mashhoor hai, to card ke notes mein 'Baraat waqt par rawana hogi' aur parking ki jagah likhna mehmanon ki madad karta hai.

  • Saddar, Cantt aur Chaklala: purane banquet halls aur clubs.
  • Satellite Town, Commercial Market aur Murree Road: shadi halls; parking ka note zaroori.
  • Peshawar Road, GT Road aur Adiala Road: barey marquees.
  • Bahria Town aur DHA (Pindi side): naye marquees; phase ka number likhein.

Rawalpindi ke mashhoor ilaqon ki alag guides: Saddar, Satellite Town, Chaklala, Bahria Town Rawalpindi.

Rawalpindi mein digital invitation card kyun

Pindi mein biradri ke 500 mehman aam hain, aur printed card baantne mein hafte lagte hain. Ek link ek din mein sab tak pohanch jata hai, aur agar Baraat ka waqt ya marquee badle to kisi ko dobara phone karne ki zaroorat nahi.

  • Hall ka naam aur address ek tap par Google Maps mein khulta hai, rasta poochne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Mehndi, Baraat aur Walima ki alag tareekh aur waqt, har function ka apna calendar button.
  • Venue ya waqt badle to pehle bheje gaye sab link khud update ho jate hain; naya card chhapwana nahi parta.
  • Har family ko private guest link: un ka naam, seats aur sirf woh functions jin mein woh invited hain.
  • Urdu aur English dono, ek button se zubaan badal jati hai.

Shehar se bahar aur overseas mehman

Pindi ke bahut se khandanon ke log fauj, Gulf aur UK mein hain, aur rishtedar Chakwal, Jhelum, Attock aur Azad Kashmir se aate hain. Card par har function ka map aur calendar button un ke liye safar ki planning aasaan kar deta hai.

Rawalpindi: aam sawalat

  • Biradri ke barey khandanon ko ek link ya alag? Har ghar ke liye private link se un ki seats likhi ja sakti hain, taake 'kitne log' ka sawal hi na rahe.
  • Islamabad ke mehman bhi hain? Card ek hi hai; Islamabad ki guide yahan.
  • Kya Rawalpindi mein koi office aana zaroori hai? Nahi, sab kuch online hai; card phone par banta hai aur WhatsApp par jata hai, chahe aap Rawalpindi mein hon ya bahar.
  • Printed card bhi chahiye? Bahut se khandan bazurgon ke liye chand printed card aur baqi sab ke liye link bhejte hain; hisaab printed vs e-invite cost mein hai.
