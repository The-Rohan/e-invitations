Digital invitation card services in Karachi, Pakistan: online shadi card ki mukammal guide

Karachi mein digital invitation card kaise banwayein: kya milta hai, qeemat kaise banti hai, DHA se Gulshan tak ke venues ke liye map, WhatsApp par bhejne ka tareeqa aur sahi service kaise chunein.

Karachi Pakistan ka sab se bara shehar hai, aur yahan shadi ka matlab hai bari guest list, kai functions, aur shehar ke ek kone se doosre kone tak ka safar. Isi liye 'digital invitation card services in Karachi' ki talash barhti ja rahi hai: log aisa card chahte hain jo WhatsApp par ek link ki tarah jaye, us mein hall ka map ho, har function ka waqt ho, aur badalne par sab ke paas khud update ho jaye. Yeh guide batati hai ke Karachi mein digital invitation card service se kya milna chahiye, qeemat kaise banti hai aur card kaise banta hai.

Digital invitation card service kya hai?

Digital invitation card ek web page hai jis ka apna link hota hai. Mehman WhatsApp par link kholta hai to phone ke browser mein card khulta hai, koi app nahi chahiye. Card par doolha dulhan ke naam, har function (Dholki, Mayun, Mehndi, Nikkah, Baraat, Walima) ki tareekh aur waqt, hall ka map, countdown, family ke rabta numbers aur RSVP hota hai. Video ya PDF card se yeh farq hai ke yeh 'zinda' hai; farq video, PDF ya asal page mein tafseel se hai.

Karachi mein service se kya milna chahiye

  • Urdu aur English dono zubaanon mein card, ek button se badalne wala; Urdu Nastaliq font mein.
  • Har function ka alag venue aur Google Maps pin; Karachi mein yeh sab se zaroori hai.
  • Calendar button: mehman tareekh apne phone ke calendar mein jod leta hai.
  • RSVP: kaun aa raha hai aur kitne log, seedha aap ke panel mein.
  • Private guest links: har family ka apna link, un ka naam aur seats.
  • Edit ki azadi: aap khud raat 12 baje bhi hall ya waqt badal sakein, kisi ka intezar na karna pare.
  • Saaf qeemat: ek dafa ki payment, koi monthly fee nahi.

Karachi mein shadi ka season aur waqt
