Digital invitation card in Gujranwala: online shadi card ki guide (گوجرانوالہ)

Gujranwala ki bari biradri wali shadiyon aur GT Road ke marquees ke liye online card: sahi map, khane ka waqt aur har ghar ke liye seats.

Gujranwala ki shadi apni ronaq aur khane ki wajah se mashhoor hai, aur biradri Wazirabad, Kamoke, Hafizabad aur Lahore tak phaili hoti hai. 'Digital invitation card Gujranwala' dhoondne wale khandan chahte hain ke itne mehmanon ko ek hi din sahi maloomat mil jaye.

Gujranwala mein shadi ka season aur venues

Gujranwala ke barey marquees GT Road ke dono taraf, Sialkot Bypass, Pasrur Road aur DC Road par hain, aur Satellite Town aur Model Town mein shadi halls. GT Road par ek jaise naam ke kai marquees hain, isliye card par map pin aur 'Lahore ki taraf / Wazirabad ki taraf' likhna mehmanon ki bari madad karta hai.

  • GT Road (Lahore aur Wazirabad dono taraf): barey marquees.
  • Satellite Town, Model Town aur Peoples Colony: shadi halls.
  • Sialkot Bypass aur Pasrur Road: marquees aur lawns.
  • DC Road aur Civil Lines: purane halls.

Gujranwala mein digital invitation card kyun

Gujranwala ki shadiyon mein 600 se zyada mehman aam hain. Ek link sab ko ek din mein mil jata hai, aur private links se har ghar ki seats likh dene se 'kitne log aayenge' ka andaza catering se pehle ho jata hai.

  • Hall ka naam aur address ek tap par Google Maps mein khulta hai, rasta poochne ki zaroorat nahi.
  • Mehndi, Baraat aur Walima ki alag tareekh aur waqt, har function ka apna calendar button.
  • Venue ya waqt badle to pehle bheje gaye sab link khud update ho jate hain; naya card chhapwana nahi parta.
  • Har family ko private guest link: un ka naam, seats aur sirf woh functions jin mein woh invited hain.
  • Urdu aur English dono, ek button se zubaan badal jati hai.

Shehar se bahar aur overseas mehman

Gujranwala ke bahut se log Spain, Italy, UK aur Gulf mein hain. Card ka calendar button aur map unke liye kaafi hai, aur Lahore ya Sialkot airport se rasta ek tap par.

Gujranwala ke liye tayyar WhatsApp message

Assalam o Alaikum! [Doolha] ki Baraat aur Walima [tareekhein] ko Gujranwala mein hain. GT Road par marquee ka sahi map is link par hai: [link]. Aap ka intezar rahega. – [Naam] aur ghar wale

Card kaise banayein: 4 qadam

  • Collection se design chunein aur apne naam ke saath muft try karein.
  • Panel mein functions, tareekh, hall aur family contacts likhein; Urdu mazmoon ke liye شادی کارڈ کا مضمون se copy karein.
  • Card aur guest pack ki payment bank transfer se; qeemat pricing par hai.
  • Link WhatsApp par bhejein. Waqt kam ho to hamari setup service sab kuch khud bhar deti hai.

Gujranwala: aam sawalat

  • GT Road par marquee dhoondna mushkil? Map pin lagayein; mehman ek tap par seedha pohanch jate hain.
  • Lahore ke mehman bhi hain? Lahore ki guide bhi dekhein.
  • Kya Gujranwala mein koi office aana zaroori hai? Nahi, sab kuch online hai; card phone par banta hai aur WhatsApp par jata hai, chahe aap Gujranwala mein hon ya bahar.
  • Printed card bhi chahiye? Bahut se khandan bazurgon ke liye chand printed card aur baqi sab ke liye link bhejte hain; hisaab printed vs e-invite cost mein hai.
