Questions

Answered before you ask.

A person reads every message, usually the same day.

What exactly am I buying?+

A digital invitation on its own web page, plus a panel where you edit it. It comes with one general link you can send to anybody. Private links addressed to each guest by name are an upgrade you can add whenever you like. Nobody has to install anything.

What is a private guest link?+

A separate address for the same invitation, made for one guest or one household. It greets them by name, states how many places are held for them, and shows only the functions they are invited to. Private links come in packs; anyone you do not have one for can still be sent the general link.

Can I change things after sending the link?+

Yes. Edits go live immediately on every link you have already shared, so you never have to re-send anything.

Is it a subscription?+

No, you pay once. The card is one price and includes 3 months of access during which it stays live and editable. Private links are extra, in packs of 30 (Rs. 1,500), 120 (Rs. 3,000) or Unlimited (Rs. 5,000), and you only buy one if you want them.
