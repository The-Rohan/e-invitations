Paying

How paying works.

Bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. A person confirms every payment.

Is it a subscription?+

No, you pay once. The card is one price and includes 3 months of access during which it stays live and editable. Private links are extra, in packs of 30 (Rs. 1,500), 120 (Rs. 3,000) or Unlimited (Rs. 5,000), and you only buy one if you want them.

How do I pay?+

By bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Checkout shows the account details; you send the amount, enter the transaction reference, and we activate your card as soon as we see it, usually within a few hours during the day.

What if I need more private links?+

Move up a pack and pay only the difference between the two, never the card again. Anyone beyond your pack can still be sent the general link, so a growing list never stops you inviting somebody.

What happens when my access period ends?+

After 3 months the link stops opening and editing pauses. Nothing is deleted. You can extend for Rs. 1,500 and pick up exactly where you left off.

Can you set it up for me?+

Yes. For Rs. 1,500 we enter everything from the details you send: names, dates, venues, photos and the guest list. You still get the panel afterwards and can change anything yourself.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. Buy a domain from any registrar and connect it from your settings; we send you the two records to add. Your original link keeps working either way.
