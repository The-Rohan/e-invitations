How it works

What exactly am I buying?+

A digital invitation on its own web page, plus a panel where you edit it. It comes with one general link you can send to anybody. Private links addressed to each guest by name are an upgrade you can add whenever you like. Nobody has to install anything.

What is a private guest link?+

A separate address for the same invitation, made for one guest or one household. It greets them by name, states how many places are held for them, and shows only the functions they are invited to. Private links come in packs; anyone you do not have one for can still be sent the general link.

Do my guests need an app or an account?+

No. They tap the link and the invitation opens in their phone's browser. That is the whole experience.

How long does it take to set up?+

Most people have something they are happy to send within an hour. Everything stays editable afterwards, so nothing has to be final on the first pass.

Is the card in English or Urdu?+

Both. The labels on the card (countdown, programme, families, questions) switch between English and Urdu with a button, and you can type your own text in either. Guests choose the language they prefer.

Editing and events

Can I change things after sending the link?+

Yes. Edits go live immediately on every link you have already shared, so you never have to re-send anything.

How many functions can one invitation have?+

As many as you are holding. Dholki, Mayun, Mehndi, Nikkah, Baraat, Walima, Aqeeqah, a birthday party: add each one with its own date, timing, venue and guest list, and call it whatever your family calls it.

Can I use my own photographs?+

Yes. Add portraits of the couple and a gallery from your panel. If you would rather not use photographs, every design has its own artwork that takes your colours.

Can I change the colours?+

Yes. Every design ships in several palettes, and you can set your own two colours from the panel. Try them on the design page before you buy.

Can I add music?+

Yes. Upload a track or paste a link and it plays when the guest opens the envelope, with a button to pause it.

Guests and sharing

How do I add a few hundred guests?+

A guest list needs a private-link pack; without one your card has its general link and nothing to manage. With a pack, add guests one at a time or paste a whole list, and count a family as one entry with a headcount rather than as separate people.

How do guests reply?+

On the card itself. Every invitation has a reply section where a guest taps coming or not coming and says how many are attending, with a short message if they like. On a private link the guest is already known by name; on the general link they type their name. Every reply lands in your panel with a running count, and the family contacts on the card are still there for anyone who would rather call.

Can I see who has opened it?+

Yes. Your panel shows the guest list, which private links have been opened, who has replied coming or not coming, and running headcounts per function.

How do I send the links on WhatsApp?+

Every guest in your list has a WhatsApp button beside their name. Tap it and a message opens with their private link already inside. The general link you can paste anywhere.

Paying and keeping it

Is it a subscription?+

No, you pay once. The card is one price and includes 3 months of access during which it stays live and editable. Private links are extra, in packs of 30 (Rs. 1,500), 120 (Rs. 3,000) or Unlimited (Rs. 5,000), and you only buy one if you want them.

How do I pay?+

By bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa. Checkout shows the account details; you send the amount, enter the transaction reference, and we activate your card as soon as we see it, usually within a few hours during the day.

What if I need more private links?+

Move up a pack and pay only the difference between the two, never the card again. Anyone beyond your pack can still be sent the general link, so a growing list never stops you inviting somebody.

What happens when my access period ends?+

After 3 months the link stops opening and editing pauses. Nothing is deleted. You can extend for Rs. 1,500 and pick up exactly where you left off.

Can you set it up for me?+

Yes. For Rs. 1,500 we enter everything from the details you send: names, dates, venues, photos and the guest list. You still get the panel afterwards and can change anything yourself.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes. Buy a domain from any registrar and connect it from your settings; we send you the two records to add. Your original link keeps working either way.
