Writing a Nikah Invitation: A Practical Guide

The Nikah invitation carries more weight than any other card of the week. What to include, what to leave out, and how to keep the tone right.

AT

Ayesha Tariq

Wedding Content Lead

نکاح

Of all the functions, the Nikah is the one guests treat with the most seriousness — and the one where an invitation's tone can go wrong most easily. Too casual and it jars. Too ornate and it obscures the details people need.

Open with Bismillah

Above the names, above everything. It sets the register instantly and is expected by almost every Pakistani family.

Name the hosts correctly

A Nikah invitation is traditionally issued by the families, not the couple. If the bride's family is hosting, that should be evident from the wording. Where both families host jointly, say so — it signals a great deal about the relationship between them.

On the ordering of names

Conventionally the groom's name comes first on a Valima invitation and the bride's on a Baraat invitation, because each names the hosting family's side. DawatNama lets you set the order explicitly rather than guessing — and it carries through to the invitation's web address.

Include a verse or a dua, but only one

Ar-Rum 30:21 is the near-universal choice, and for good reason. Stacking multiple verses crowds the design and dilutes the effect. One verse, given room to breathe, above or below the names.

Be precise about the timing

  • State the Nikah time clearly — this ceremony genuinely starts near the stated time, unlike most functions.
  • If the Nikah is followed immediately by the Baraat, say so, so guests know how long to plan for.
  • Mention if the ceremony is at a mosque, and whether prayer arrangements are available.
  • Note separate seating arrangements if applicable — guests appreciate knowing in advance.

What to leave off

No gift registry. No dress code, unless it is genuinely necessary. No hashtag on the Nikah invitation — save it for the Mehndi card, where the tone can carry it.

Close with dua

Then the contact numbers, then the map link, then nothing else. A Nikah invitation is at its best when it is short.
