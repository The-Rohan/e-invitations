A WhatsApp wedding invitation that is more than a forwarded photo

Most Pakistani wedding invitations travel by WhatsApp, so a digital card should be built for it: one link that previews nicely, opens fast on any phone and tells you who has opened it.

One link instead of many attachments

Send the link and WhatsApp shows a preview with your names. Guests tap it and the card opens in their browser. When something changes, you fix it once and the same link shows the new version.

Personal links for each family

Instead of one link for everyone, give each family its own. It greets them by name, keeps the seats you reserved, shows only the functions they are invited to and tells you when they opened it and what they replied.

A picture for status and groups

Some people will always prefer a picture. You can download a ready-made picture of your card with a QR code on it, for family groups, status and printed cards.

A few habits that help

  • Send it in the evening, when people are more likely to open it with their family.
  • Send a short message of your own with the link, so it does not look like a mass forward.
  • Send a gentle reminder to the families who have not replied a week before the deadline.

Questions people ask

Will WhatsApp show a preview with our names?

Yes. The link carries your names and the date, so it appears as a preview card in the chat.

Can I see who has opened it?

With personal guest links you see when each family opened their invitation and what they replied.

See it on your own phone

Build your card, preview it privately and decide afterwards. Nothing goes online and nobody else can see it during the free trial.
