Digital versus paper wedding cards: an honest comparison

Neither is better in every case. Paper carries tradition; digital carries information that changes. Many families use both.

Try it free for 3 days Open a live demo ↗

Where paper is better

  • It is tangible, and for some elders it is the only invitation that counts.
  • It is a keepsake, and handing one over is a gesture in itself.
  • It does not depend on a phone, a connection or a link.

Where digital is better

  • It reaches relatives abroad in seconds, with times in their own time zone.
  • It can be corrected: a changed venue or time is fixed once.
  • It carries the map, the programme, the calendar entry and the RSVP in one place.
  • It tells you who is coming, per function, as the replies arrive.

The combination many families choose

Print a simple card for elders and close family, and put a QR code on it that opens the digital card. Everyone has the tradition and the live details. If anything changes, the QR code still works.

Questions people ask

Do I have to give up paper cards?

No. A QR code on a paper card links to the digital one, so you can use both.

What if some guests do not use WhatsApp?

The card is a normal web link, so it can be sent by SMS, email or a QR code as well.
