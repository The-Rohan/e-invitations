Free trial terms

  1. The trial is free for 3 days from the moment you open your account. You do not need to pay to start.
  2. Your preview is private. The preview address of your card opens only for you while you are signed in. It is not published, not shared and not listed in search engines.
  3. After the trial your account is locked until you pay. Nothing is deleted: when your payment is approved, your account opens again exactly where you left it.
  4. Payment is in two steps: an advance of 50% of the package to unlock your account, and the balance before your card goes online. Pay by bank transfer; our team approves each payment after checking it. Your card goes online when the full amount has been approved.
  5. Custom designs. A ready-made design has no extra charge. Customising one of our designs costs Rs 1,500; a design made from your own idea costs Rs 3,000. The design charge is paid in advance together with the 50% advance of the package; the rest of the package is due before the card goes online. Until you send a payment you can change or remove the design choice yourself in your account.
  6. Reversing a design charge and refunds. If you decide not to go ahead with a custom design, tell us and we reverse the design charge: it is taken off your total, and whatever you already paid for it is sent back to you by bank transfer (or another way we agree with you). The refund appears in your payment history as “Sent back to you”, and we message you when it has been sent. Nothing you paid is ever deleted from your history, and the rest of your package and your card are not affected.
  7. Your card is hosted on orders.sehra.pk for the period your package includes. After it goes online, the package's revision rules apply.
  8. Your details (name, WhatsApp number, card content, payment receipts) are used only to run your account and are never sold. Payment receipts and design references are stored privately and only our admin can open them.
  9. One free trial per WhatsApp number.
