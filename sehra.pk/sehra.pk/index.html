Questions

Good to know

Can anyone see my card during the trial?

No. The preview address only opens for you, while you are signed in. Anyone else sees "not found". It is also hidden from Google.

What happens after 3 days?

Your account is locked, not deleted. Pay when you are ready and the admin approves your payment: your account opens again and everything is exactly where you left it.

How do I pay?

In two steps: an advance of 50% of the package to unlock your account (plus the design charge if you asked for a custom design), then the balance when you are happy and want the card online. Pay by bank transfer; send a screenshot or the transaction ID on the payment page. We approve it, usually within a few hours.

How do custom designs work?

Tell us what you want and upload any reference pictures. The design charge (Rs 1,500 to customise one of our designs, Rs 3,000 for your own design) is paid in advance together with the 50% advance of your package. Our designer starts once it is approved; you pay the balance before the card goes online.

Can I remove the background song?

Yes. In the card builder, open Background music and press Remove song (or choose "No music").

Where will my card live?

On orders.sehra.pk, at an address made from your names, for as long as your package includes.
