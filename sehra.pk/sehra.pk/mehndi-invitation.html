Mehndi invitation cards as bright as the night itself

The Mehndi is the loudest, most colourful night of the wedding, and its invitation should feel that way. Give it its own card, its own link and its own guest list.

What a Mehndi card needs to say

  • The date, the time guests should arrive and the venue, with a map button so nobody gets lost on the way.
  • A dress code or colour idea, such as yellow and green, so the room looks like one family.
  • The programme in a line or two: dhol, dances, the henna ceremony, dinner.
  • A clear RSVP, because the Mehndi guest list is often different from the Baraat list.

A card of its own, linked to the others

Many families invite a different group to each function. With a separate Mehndi card the Mehndi link carries only the Mehndi details, only the people you send it to open it, and replies are counted for the Mehndi alone. The cards of one wedding can still point to each other, so a guest invited to two functions moves between them in one tap.

How it opens

Mehndi cards start from a warm marigold theme and an opening built around a diya, with petals and a little music. Every part is changeable: pick another theme, another opening, your own wording and your own song.

Questions people ask

Can I send the Mehndi card separately from the wedding card?

Yes. Each function can have its own card with its own link, look, venue and guest list. The cards of one wedding can be linked to each other so guests see the whole programme.

Can guests reply for the Mehndi only?

Yes. RSVP is counted per function, so you see exactly how many people are coming to the Mehndi and how many to the other nights.

How do I share it?

You get one private link. Paste it in WhatsApp and it opens in any phone browser. Nothing to install. The card is never listed on Google.
