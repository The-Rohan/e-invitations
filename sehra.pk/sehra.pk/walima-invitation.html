Walima invitation cards for the day after the wedding

The Walima is the groom’s family’s celebration, usually larger and more relaxed than the days before it. Its card can be softer, and it needs its own list of guests.

What makes the Walima card different

  • It is hosted by the groom’s family, so the host line and the wording change.
  • The guest list is often the widest of the whole wedding: relatives, colleagues, neighbours.
  • It is usually a lunch or dinner, so a clear time and a headcount matter to the caterer.
  • The mood is gentler: pastel and floral themes, a curtain or a letter opening instead of a fanfare.

Keep the guest lists separate

A Walima-only card goes to the people invited to the Walima and nobody else. Replies are counted for the Walima alone, so you can plan the numbers without mixing them with the Mehndi or the Baraat.

Wording you can borrow

With gratitude and joy,
the family of Mr and Mrs Siddiqui invites you to the Walima of Hamza and Ayesha.
Sunday, 13 December, dinner at 8 pm.

Questions people ask

Can the Walima card have a different look from the Baraat card?

Yes. Every card in a wedding can have its own theme and opening, and they can still be linked together.

Can I invite some guests only to the Walima?

Yes. Send the Walima link only to those guests; or use personal guest links, which can limit which functions each family sees.
