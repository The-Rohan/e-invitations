Nikah invitation cards that keep the dua at the centre

The Nikah is the heart of the wedding and its invitation should be calm, clear and respectful: the time, the place, and a line of dua.

A quieter design for the most important moment

Choose an understated theme in ivory, sage or soft green, switch on the Bismillah banner and add a verse such as Ar-Rum 30:21. The opening animation is optional, so a very simple card is just as welcome as a cinematic one.

Practical details to include

  • The exact time of the Nikah, and when guests should be seated.
  • The venue, with a map link and a note about the entrance if the venue is a home or a masjid.
  • A line about seating arrangements for women and men, if your family follows separate seating.
  • Whether the Nikah is only for family or open to friends too.
  • Names of the host families, and a note if guests are expected to stay for dinner or lunch.

Wording you can borrow

In the name of Allah, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful.
With the blessings of our families, we invite you to witness the Nikah of Maryam and Hassan, Friday 11 December, at 3 pm. Your duas are the best gift.

Questions people ask

Can I add a Quranic verse and the Bismillah?

Yes. Both can be switched on or off in your card, and the verse can be changed.

Can I make it a very simple card?

Yes. Pick a calm theme and skip the animated opening; guests go straight to the details.
