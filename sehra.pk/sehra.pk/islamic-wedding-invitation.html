Islamic wedding invitations with Bismillah and dua

A wedding is a sunnah, and the invitation can begin the way the day will: with the name of Allah, a verse and a request for dua.

Try it free for 3 days Open a live demo ↗

What can be included

  • A Bismillah banner at the top of the card, which you can switch on or off.
  • A verse such as Ar-Rum 30:21, in Arabic with a translation, in the invitation wording.
  • A request for duas, and a wishes wall where guests leave theirs.
  • Modest, calm themes in ivory, sage and soft green, and an opening that is simple or absent.

Respectful practical details

Use the event notes to say when the Nikah will take place, how seating is arranged for women and men if your family follows separate seating, and whether music is part of the programme. You decide how much to say.

Music is your choice

Background music is optional and can be muted by the guest. You can leave it off completely, or use a gentle instrumental track.

Questions people ask

Can the card be completely without music?

Yes. Music is optional, and every guest can also mute it.

Can I change the verse?

Yes. The verse is part of your invitation wording and you can replace it.
