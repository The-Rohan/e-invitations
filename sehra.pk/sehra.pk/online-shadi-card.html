An online shadi card your guests open, not just scroll past

An online shadi card is a wedding invitation that lives at a private link instead of on paper or in a photo. Guests tap the link, and the invitation opens in their phone browser with every detail in one place.

Try it free for 3 days Open a live demo ↗

How it differs from a photo or a PDF

  • A photo is a picture of a card. It cannot show a map, add the date to a calendar, play music or count replies.
  • A PDF has to be downloaded, is hard to read on a phone and goes out of date the moment a venue changes.
  • An online card is a page. Fix a mistake or change a venue once and every guest sees the new version at the same link.

What a good one includes

  • Your names, the date and a short welcome, in English and Urdu, with a one-tap switch.
  • Every function with its own time, venue and map, and an add-to-calendar button.
  • RSVP for each function, so you know who is coming where.
  • Music and a short opening that makes the card feel like an occasion.
  • Photos, a story timeline and a wishes wall, if you want them.

Privacy

The card is a private link. It is not listed on Google, on our site or anywhere else, and only the people you send it to can open it. Replies are visible only to you.

Try it before you pay

You can build your card, preview it on your own phone and change anything you like during a free three-day trial. The preview stays private to you and never goes online.

Questions people ask

Do guests need to install anything?

No. The card opens in the browser of any phone, from a WhatsApp message, with nothing to install.

Can older relatives open it?

Yes. It is one link, the Urdu is shown in clear Nastaliq for those who prefer it, and you can also share a ready-made picture of the card for family groups.

What if the venue changes?

You edit the card once, and everyone who opens the link sees the new venue. There is no need to send it again.

See it on your own phone

Build your card, preview it privately and decide afterwards. Nothing goes online and nobody else can see it during the free trial.
