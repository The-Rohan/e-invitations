Baraat invitation cards that get the arrival right

The Baraat is the moment the whole wedding turns on, and most of the questions guests ask are about timing and place. A good Baraat card answers them before anyone has to call.

The details guests look for

  • Two times, not one: when guests should arrive and when the Baraat is expected, so nobody waits an hour in a hall that is still being set up.
  • The hall and the exact entrance, with a map link that opens in the phone’s map app.
  • Parking or transport notes for a big venue.
  • The programme in order: arrival, Nikah, Rukhsati, dinner.
  • A headcount, so the hall and caterer get a real number.

One card, both families

Name both host families, add the couple’s names in English and Urdu, and let guests reply with the number of people coming. You see the live count per function, and you can export the guest list with replies as a sheet for the caterer and the hall.

A grand opening for a grand day

The Baraat card opens with a ceremonial entrance: guests break a wax seal, part the sehra or open a royal gate before the details appear. Every opening works with every theme, so you can choose the mood rather than the template.

Questions people ask

Can I put the arrival time and the Baraat time separately?

Yes. Add the time guests should arrive and the time the Baraat is expected, along with a note about parking or transport.

Can the caterer get the guest count?

Yes. You see replies per function and can export them as a sheet, including the number of guests each family is bringing.
