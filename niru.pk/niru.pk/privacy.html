Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

This policy explains how Niru handles information about customers, couples, invitation guests and visitors to niru.pk when we prepare, deliver and support a digital wedding invitation.

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address and WhatsApp number.
  • Couple and family names, wedding dates, events, venues, wording and creative preferences.
  • Photos and reference images you choose to upload.
  • Your chosen payment method and payment screenshot, when payment is requested.
  • Messages you send through WhatsApp, email or Instagram.
  • Guest RSVP responses, attendance choices, wishes and any contact details a guest chooses to submit through an invitation.

How we use it

We use this information to review and confirm an order, verify payment, create and revise the invitation, provide its private link, communicate about the order, manage RSVPs where included and provide support. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and access

Order records are stored in the studio's protected Google Workspace tools. Uploaded photos and payment screenshots are stored in a private Drive folder for the order. Access is limited to people working on or administering the order. Where an invitation includes RSVP management, the customer or couple receives a private Niru dashboard that displays guest responses from protected storage. Its secret access link must be kept with the couple. Invitation links themselves are unlisted rather than password protected: anyone who receives or forwards an invitation link may be able to open it, so customers should share it only with intended guests.

Website analytics and advertising

Niru uses first-party, privacy-safe funnel analytics to count anonymous visits and actions such as viewing a design, opening a demo, beginning an order or encountering a technical error. It records an anonymous session identifier, page, design, general device type, referral source and campaign. It does not record form answers, names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos or payment details.

Niru uses the Meta Pixel and Meta Conversions API to measure whether advertisements lead to website visits, card-demo views, order starts, WhatsApp contacts, submitted orders and confirmed purchases, and to create or exclude advertising audiences. Browser events may include technical information such as browser or device identifiers, IP address, visited page and referring page. For submitted orders and confirmed purchases, recognised contact identifiers such as a name, email address or phone number may be normalised and securely hashed before being sent to Meta through advanced matching or the server-side Conversions API to improve advertising attribution and audience matching. Niru does not intentionally send wedding dates, venues, invitation wording, photos, payment screenshots or invitation content to Meta.

Meta may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own privacy policy. Visitors can use browser controls and Meta ad preferences to limit this processing.

Spam protection

We use Google reCAPTCHA before saving a new order request to help prevent automated spam and abuse. Google may process browser and device information, IP addresses, cookies and interactions needed for this security check under its Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Our server verifies the security token before accepting a website order.

WhatsApp and external links

The floating WhatsApp button and other contact links open WhatsApp with a suggested message. Opening the link does not send the message; you choose whether to send it. If you do, WhatsApp processes your account information and message under its own terms and privacy policy. Invitation links may also open services such as maps or calendars when a visitor chooses those features.

Drafts on your device

When you submit your basic contact details, we save your order request and email a private return link. Wedding details and uploads you add afterwards are saved securely as an unfinished draft so you can continue from another device. The link gives access to your order, so keep it private. Drafts are stored using Google Drive and our order records use Google Sheets, with access restricted to the studio. Clearing browser data does not delete a saved server draft; contact us to request deletion. Payment screenshots are checked by the studio and do not automatically confirm payment.

Retention and deletion

We keep order information and uploaded files only as long as needed to provide the service, support the invitation and maintain necessary transaction records. Your invitation is hosted for one year from delivery and can remain active as a wedding memory on request. You may ask us to remove photos or other personal content when it is no longer needed, subject to records we must retain for payment, fraud prevention or legal purposes.

Service providers

We use website hosting, email, Google Sheets and Google Drive to operate the service, Meta for advertising measurement, and communication platforms such as WhatsApp and Instagram when you contact us there. Some providers may process or store information outside Pakistan under their own security and privacy arrangements.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect order information and restrict access. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so customers should avoid sending information that is not needed for the invitation.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct or delete personal information connected to your order. You may also ask which files we still hold. We will verify that the request comes from the customer or couple before acting on it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or providers change. The date above shows the latest revision.
