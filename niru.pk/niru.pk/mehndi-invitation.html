Mehndi Invitation Cards for the Night Before Everything Begins

What is a Mehndi, and what does its invitation need to say?

The Mehndi is the pre-wedding function where the bride's hands and feet are decorated with henna, usually a day or two before the Baraat: informal, danced-through, and one of the first functions on the wedding calendar. Sometimes the bride and groom hold separate Mehndis at their own homes; many families now combine them into one evening hosted jointly. Either way, the invitation should say plainly whose Mehndi it is, who is hosting (both sets of parents, or one side), the date, the venue and the time. Mehndis usually start in the early evening and run late.

Colour and mood carry more weight here than on any other card. Yellow, parrot green, hot pink, mustard and orange read "Mehndi" at a glance, and the dress code line should say so: bright traditional wear, and many families ask guests to avoid black and white. Expect dhol, dance performances, a stage for the couple and mehndi for the guests too. Keep the wording warm and lively rather than formal; this is the one function where a playful line beats a stiff one.

Wording notes

Name the hosts, name the couple (groom first, bride second), and let the colour do the rest. If there are two separate Mehndis, say so on the card rather than leaving guests guessing which house to go to, and give each its own date, time and address.

How Niru handles a Mehndi invitation

A Niru invitation is one link, opened on WhatsApp, that carries every function of the wedding, and each function gets its own section inside the card: its own date, its own venue and directions, its own dress-code line. Guests choose which functions they are attending when they RSVP, so your private response sheet shows the Mehndi headcount separately from the Baraat and the Walima.

That matters for a Mehndi more than most functions, because the Mehndi guest list often differs from the rest: close friends and cousins who will not be at the Nikah, or separate bride's-side and groom's-side crowds. Two ways to handle the split:

  • One card for everyone. Every guest opens the same link and sees every function, and replies only for the ones they are coming to.
  • A Mehndi-only link. For guests invited to nothing but the Mehndi, a separate guest-list card showing only the Mehndi costs PKR 1,500. Useful when the Mehndi really is a different crowd.

Questions people ask

Can I send a separate invitation just for the Mehndi?

Yes. Order the main invitation for your whole wedding, then add a Mehndi-only guest-list card for PKR 1,500. It is a separate link showing only the Mehndi's date, venue and dress code, for guests invited to that function alone.

What colours work best for a Mehndi invitation?

Yellow, parrot green, mustard, hot pink and orange all read as Mehndi. Chunri is built around exactly that palette, and Signature designs include a custom colour palette if you want a different base design in Mehndi colours.

Should the bride's and groom's Mehndi be on one card or two?

Either works. If both families are hosting together, one Mehndi section is simplest. If the two sides host separately, give each its own section with its own date, venue and time so no guest turns up at the wrong house.

Does the Mehndi get its own RSVP?

Yes. Guests choose the functions they are attending, so your private response sheet shows exactly who is coming to the Mehndi and who is coming to the Baraat or Walima.

Is a digital Mehndi invitation appropriate, or should I still print cards?

Most guests now receive wedding invitations on WhatsApp, and everything a printed Mehndi card carries is on the link, plus a map, a countdown and an RSVP a paper card cannot do. Many families still print a few cards for elders; see digital vs paper wedding cards.
