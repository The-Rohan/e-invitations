It is one flat price for the whole wedding: every function, unlimited guests, English and Urdu, RSVP and a year of hosting. These are founding prices for our first 50 couples; after that, the regular prices apply.

Printed card prices in Pakistan vary too widely by printer, paper, city, quantity and season to quote one honest number, so this page explains what drives the cost of any invitation and what a digital one includes.

What drives the cost of any wedding card?

Printed or digital, a few things move the price:

  • Design. Custom artwork, hand lettering or an elaborate opening costs more to make than a simple layout.
  • Quantity. Printed cards are priced per piece, so 150 cards and 600 cards cost very differently even in the same design.
  • Printing and finishing. Paper stock, foil, boxes, envelopes and any hand finishing each add cost.
  • Delivery. Printed cards have to reach every guest, often across cities.
  • Changes. A printed run is fixed once it is at the printer; any change means reprinting some or all of it.

What a digital invitation removes, and what you pay for instead

A digital invitation removes quantity, printing and delivery entirely: one link works for 50 guests or 500 at the same price. What you pay for is the design and the build: the interactive entrance, the bilingual card, RSVP with a private response sheet, maps, countdown, gallery and the rest, plus revisions after your first preview and hosting for the year the invitation is live.
