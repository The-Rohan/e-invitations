Walima Invitation Cards for the Wedding's Last Evening

What is the Walima, and what does its invitation need to say?

The Walima is the reception hosted by the groom's family, traditionally held a day or two after the Nikah and Rukhsati. It is the couple's first appearance together as husband and wife, and usually the most formally dressed function of the wedding. Where the Mehndi is colour and the Nikah is solemn, the Walima is polish: jewel tones, formal eastern wear, an evening timing, and often the widest guest list of the whole wedding, including colleagues and family friends who were not at the smaller functions.

Because the groom's family is hosting, the invitation should name them clearly. Because the Walima is often the largest gathering, timing and venue need to be precise: a start time guests can plan around, and directions to a venue that may be different from the Baraat's.

Wording notes

Walima wording is warm but composed, in the words of the groom's family, introducing the couple as newly married. Say that dinner will be served; guests expect to know.

How Niru handles a Walima invitation

Because the Walima guest list is usually the widest, it is the function most likely to need its own link: a separate guest-list card (PKR 1,500), sent only to Walima guests, showing nothing about the Mehndi or Nikah they were not invited to. Inside the main invitation the Walima has its own section with its own map, its own dress-code line and its own count on your RSVP sheet, which is what the caterer needs: the Walima headcount is very often not the Mehndi's or the Baraat's.

The photo gallery and story timeline do their best work around the Walima. By the time guests open the invitation for the reception, the couple's own photographs sit naturally beside the venue details.

Questions people ask

Can I invite people to the Walima only?

Yes. Order a Walima-only guest-list link for PKR 1,500 and those guests see only the Walima's date, venue and dress code, with no other function on their card.

What dress code should a Walima invitation state?

Formal eastern wear, usually in jewel tones, since the Walima is typically the most dressed-up function. Say it plainly so nobody arrives in Mehndi colours.

Does the Walima need its own RSVP count?

Yes. Guests choose which functions they are attending, so your response sheet shows the Walima headcount separately, which matters for catering.

Who is named as host on a Walima card?

Traditionally the groom's family. Each function on a Niru invitation can name its own hosts, so the Walima can name the groom's family while the Baraat names the bride's.

Should the Walima invitation mention dinner?

Yes. "Dinner will be served" is a normal and helpful line on a Walima card, because it tells guests how to plan their evening.
