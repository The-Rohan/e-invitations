Refund Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

Every Niru invitation is a digital product, hand crafted individually for your wedding. Once your order, price and payment are confirmed, refunds depend on the stage the work has reached.

Before we start

If you change your mind before we begin crafting your invitation, you are entitled to a full refund. Just message us with your order number.

After we start, before your preview

Once we have begun personalising your invitation but before your first preview is shared, a partial refund may be offered to cover the design time already spent. We will always be fair and explain the amount clearly.

After your preview is shared

Once we have shared your preview, the work is substantially complete, so orders at this stage are generally non-refundable. Instead, we will complete the revision rounds included with your order: up to five for a Signature Template Edition, and unlimited revisions within the approved creative direction for Signature Personalised, Premium and Atelier orders.

If we cannot deliver the agreed service

If Niru cannot provide the agreed invitation, or a material problem caused by us cannot be corrected within a reasonable time, we will offer an appropriate full or partial refund based on the work successfully delivered. This policy does not limit any right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you are charged twice or a payment does not result in an order, contact us with your payment screenshot and we will return the extra amount in full.

How to request a refund

  • Message us on WhatsApp or email with your order number.
  • Tell us briefly why you would like a refund.
  • Approved refunds are initiated to the original payment method, where practical, within 7 working days. Your bank or payment service may take additional time to display the amount.
