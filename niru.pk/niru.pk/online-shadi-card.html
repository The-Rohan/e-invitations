Online Shadi Card: A Digital Shaadi Card Your Guests Open on WhatsApp

Online shadi card kya hota hai?

An online shadi card is a wedding invitation sent as a link instead of a printed card or a forwarded photo. A Niru shadi card is one private link you send on WhatsApp; guests open it on their phone, with no app, and find every function inside it: the date, time, venue, map and dress code for each one, in English and Urdu.

It is not a PDF and not a video file. Every Niru design opens with a small moment the guest does by hand: pressing and holding a diya, knocking three times on a haveli door, breaking a wax seal, drawing a chunri aside. Then the card itself, with music behind it.

Printed card ya online card: what actually changes?

  • No printing, no delivery. Fifty guests or five hundred, it is the same link and the same price.
  • Changes don't mean reprinting. If a time or a venue changes, it is updated on the same link, so the card on every guest's phone is the current one. (The Nikah and Baraat dates are locked once the card is created.)
  • Guests reply inside the card. Each guest chooses which functions they are coming to, and you get a private live response sheet with the count for each function.
  • Urdu and English in one card. Elders read it in Urdu, set in Nastaliq; the cousin abroad reads it in English. One tap switches the whole card.

Many families still print a small run of cards for the elders they visit in person. A digital card covers everyone else; our digital vs paper guide goes through that choice honestly.

Card mein kya kya hota hai?

Every Niru shadi card includes:

  • A hand-opened entrance, different for every design
  • English and Urdu in the same card
  • Background music, with a mute button
  • Venue maps and directions for every function, and one-tap add to calendar
  • A live countdown and a scratch-to-reveal date
  • A photo gallery and a guest wishes wall
  • A dress code line for each function
  • RSVP with a private live response sheet for the couple
  • Optional Bismillah, the Ar-Rum 30:21 verse, a purdah seating note and a Nikah registration note

Shadi card kitne ka banta hai?

One price covers the whole wedding: every function and unlimited guests. Signature is PKR 5,900, Premium is PKR 8,400 and Atelier is PKR 12,490. These are founding prices for our first 50 couples; after that, the regular prices apply. A separate guest-list card, for example a Walima-only link, is PKR 1,500, and a custom address such as taha-ayesha.niru.pk is PKR 1,000. The price guide has the full breakdown, tier by tier.

Online shadi card kaise banwayein?

  1. Choose a design. Open any live design on your phone and go through it the way a guest would.
  2. Send your details. Names, families, functions, venues, wording and photos go into the order form.
  3. Pay. In full, or 50% now and 50% after your first draft, by bank transfer, Easypaisa or SadaPay.
  4. See your first preview. Signature in 24 to 48 hours, Premium in 2 to 3 days, Atelier in 4 to 5 days, once details and payment are confirmed.
  5. Revise, then send. Signature templates include up to five revision rounds; Premium and Atelier are unlimited within the approved direction. Then the link goes out on WhatsApp.

Questions people ask

Online shadi card kaise bhejte hain?

You send the card's link on WhatsApp, to each guest, a broadcast list or a family group. Guests tap the link and the card opens in their phone's browser, with no app to install. WhatsApp shows a preview with the couple's names and an image.

Kya shadi card WhatsApp par bhejna theek hai?

For most guests, yes, and it puts the map and timings for every function on their phone. For elders you would visit in person, many families still hand over a printed card and send the link as well, so the map and timings are on their phone.

Shadi card kitne din mein ready hota hai?

The first preview arrives in 24 to 48 hours for Signature, 2 to 3 days for Premium and 4 to 5 days for Atelier, counted from when your details and payment are confirmed. Revisions follow from there.

Kya Mehndi ya Walima ka alag card ban sakta hai?

Yes. Order the main card for the whole wedding, then add a separate guest-list card for PKR 1,500. It is a second link showing only the functions those guests are invited to, in the same design.

Card iPhone aur Android dono par chalta hai?

Yes. It opens in any modern phone browser, on iPhone and Android, and on tablets and computers too.

Card kitne arse tak live rehta hai?

One year from delivery, and longer on request, so it can stay up as a memory of the wedding.
