FAQ

Questions families ask

How many designs can I choose from?

There are 35 animated designs right now, from royal maroon and Islamic geometry to mehndi colours, walima pastels and kids’ themes, and more are being added. In the studio, pick your occasion and filter by style or colour.

Do I have to pay before I see my invitation?

No. Make your invitation and watch the full animated preview for free, without signing up. You pay only when you are happy with it. Until then the preview carries a “PREVIEW” watermark.

Is it safe to pay by Easypaisa?

You see your full preview free before paying. Then you send the amount to the Easypaisa account (IBAN) shown on your order page, from Easypaisa, JazzCash or any bank app, and tell us the name of the account you paid from. Your order page updates by itself once the payment is confirmed, and if your invitation is not delivered within 24 hours of a verified payment you get a full refund.

How do you know my payment is mine?

We match it by the amount and the name of the account it came from, so after paying you just type that name on your order page. Your receipt with its transaction ID stays your proof of payment.

How do guests open the invitation?

You get a link such as dawatlink.com/dawat/ali-ayesha-k3d9. Guests tap it in WhatsApp and it opens in their phone’s browser. No app, no download, no signup. It works on Android and iPhone.

Can I write the names in Urdu?

Yes. Choose English, Urdu or both. Type names and lines with your phone’s Urdu keyboard and they appear in Nastaliq script on the card and on the invitation page.

Can I make changes after paying?

Yes. Before payment you can change everything as often as you like. After payment, Web Invite and Link + Video include 1 edit and Premium includes 3. Your link stays the same after an edit.

How do I put the video on my WhatsApp status?

With Link + Video or Premium, download the 9:16 MP4 from your order page and post it as your status. The video is made on your own phone, so open your order page in Chrome on Android or Safari on iPhone to download it.

Can I choose Bismillah or 786 at the top?

Yes. Pick Bismillah, Bismihi Ta‘ala, 786 or nothing at all. Many families prefer Bismihi Ta‘ala or 786 on cards that are shared widely.

How long does my link stay live?

At least 12 months from your payment, so guests can open it before the event and look back at it afterwards.

Can guests reply (RSVP)?

If you add a WhatsApp number, guests get a button to reply to you on WhatsApp with a ready-made message. Premium also adds an RSVP form on the invitation and a private guest list with head count and CSV download.

Can I call you?

We don’t take calls. Use Help or WhatsApp messages; we reply within a few hours.

What is your refund policy?

You get a full refund if your invitation is not delivered within 24 hours of a verified payment, or if there is a technical fault we cannot fix. We cannot refund after delivery for a change of mind; use your included edits instead.
