FAQ’s

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What kind of invitations do you offer?

Digital invitations that open as their own web page, for Nikkah, Mehndi, Baraat, Walima, engagements, birthdays and other celebrations. Many designs are animated or filmed, and every invitation can also be saved as a PDF.

2. Can I customize the invitation?

Yes, all of it: names, dates, timings, venues with map links, family names, photos, music, which pages show, and the wording on every line, in English and Urdu. You edit it yourself from your own dashboard, and every change goes live on links you have already sent.

3. How do I choose a design?

Open any design to see it working, then press "Try it for Free" to see it with your own names before you pay. Only pay when you are happy with it.

4. Can I request a custom invitation?

Yes. Tell us your colours, theme and story on WhatsApp and we design one for you from scratch (from Rs. 25,000).

5. Are these digital invitations?

Yes. Your guests tap the link and the invitation opens in their phone's browser. There is no app to install and no account to make, and it works on every phone, tablet and computer.

6. Which wedding events can I include?

As many as you are having: Dholki, Mayun, Mehndi, Nikkah, Baraat, Walima, Qawwali and more. Each has its own date, time, venue and map, and its own link, so you can send the Walima to some guests and every function to others.
